# MHC Epitope Analysis

## Overview

This folder contains the results of **MHC-I and MHC-II epitope analysis** performed for the in silico multi-epitope subunit vaccine design targeting the **LptE protein of multidrug-resistant *Acinetobacter baumannii***.

MHC epitope analysis was performed to identify potential **T-cell epitopes** that may be suitable for incorporation into the multi-epitope vaccine construct.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis were to:

* Identify potential MHC-I and MHC-II binding peptide epitopes.
* Evaluate the predicted immunogenicity of the candidate epitopes.
* Evaluate the allergenic potential of the predicted epitopes.
* Shortlist suitable epitopes for further vaccine design.

## MHC-I Epitope Analysis

MHC-I epitopes were evaluated for their ability to interact with MHC class I molecules and their potential to stimulate an immune response.

The predicted MHC-I peptides were further evaluated for:

* MHC-I binding properties
* Immunogenicity using VaxiJen 3.0
* Allergenicity using AllerTOP v2.1

Peptides meeting the defined selection criteria were considered for further analysis and vaccine construct development.

### MHC-I Results

The complete MHC-I analysis is available in:

`MHC_I_Epitopes.xlsx`

The Excel file contains the predicted peptide sequences along with their immunogenicity and allergenicity results.

## MHC-II Epitope Analysis

MHC-II epitopes were evaluated for their potential interaction with MHC class II molecules and their potential contribution to the immune response.

The predicted MHC-II peptides were further evaluated for:

* MHC-II binding properties
* Immunogenicity
* Allergenicity

Suitable candidates were shortlisted according to the defined selection criteria.

### MHC-II Results

The complete MHC-II analysis is available in:

`MHC_II_Epitopes.xlsx`

The Excel file contains the predicted peptide sequences along with their immunogenicity and allergenicity results.

## Epitope Selection Criteria

Candidate epitopes were screened using multiple parameters. The major criteria included:

* Favorable MHC binding
* High predicted immunogenicity
* Non-allergenic prediction
* Suitability for further vaccine construct development

For the current project, **100% predicted immunogenic peptides were prioritized according to the selection criterion provided by the project mentor**.

Peptides with lower predicted immunogenicity were retained in the complete results for documentation but were not prioritized for final selection unless otherwise instructed.
