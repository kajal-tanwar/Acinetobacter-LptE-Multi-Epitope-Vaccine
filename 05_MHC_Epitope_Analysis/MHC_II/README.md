# MHC-II Epitope Analysis

## Overview

This folder contains the results of **MHC-II epitope analysis** performed as part of the in silico multi-epitope subunit vaccine design targeting the **LptE protein of multidrug-resistant *Acinetobacter baumannii***.

The analysis was performed to identify potential **MHC-II binding peptide epitopes** that may serve as candidate helper T-lymphocyte (HTL) epitopes for further vaccine design.

## MHC-II Epitope Prediction

MHC-II epitopes were predicted from the selected LptE protein sequence using an MHC-II epitope prediction approach.

The predicted peptides were evaluated based on their MHC-II binding properties and subsequently screened using additional immunoinformatics analyses.

## Immunogenicity Analysis

The predicted MHC-II peptides were evaluated for their predicted immunogenicity.

For this project, peptides showing **100% predicted immunogenicity** were prioritized according to the selection criterion provided by the project mentor.

Peptides with lower predicted immunogenicity were retained in the complete results for documentation but were not prioritized for final selection.

## Allergenicity Analysis

The MHC-II peptide candidates were evaluated for allergenic potential using **AllergenFP**.

Peptides predicted as **Non-allergen** were considered suitable for further screening.

## Final Selection Criteria

MHC-II peptides were prioritized for further vaccine design based on the following criteria:

* Favorable MHC-II binding properties
* **100% predicted immunogenicity**
* **Non-allergenic** prediction
* Suitability for subsequent vaccine construct development

## Results

The complete MHC-II epitope analysis, including peptide sequences, immunogenicity results, and allergenicity results, is provided in:

`MHC_II_Results.xlsx`

The Excel file contains the evaluated MHC-II peptides and their corresponding screening results.

## Summary

The MHC-II epitope analysis identified candidate helper T-cell epitopes from the LptE protein for potential inclusion in the multi-epitope vaccine construct.

Peptides satisfying the defined selection criteria were prioritized for subsequent stages of vaccine design.
