# MHC Class I Epitope Prediction Results

## Protein Information

- **Protein:** LPS-assembly lipoprotein LptE
- **Organism:** *Acinetobacter baumannii*
- **Protein Length:** 169 amino acids

---

## Tool Used

- **Tool:** Immune Epitope Database (IEDB) MHC-I Binding Prediction
- **Website:** https://www.iedb.org/
- **Prediction Method:** IEDB Recommended
- **Epitope Length:** 9-mer
- **Allele Set:** Human HLA Class I Reference Set

---

## Objective

To identify potential MHC Class I (CD8⁺ T-cell) epitopes from the LptE protein that can stimulate cytotoxic T-cell immune responses for inclusion in a multi-epitope subunit vaccine.

## Interpretation

The IEDB MHC-I Binding Prediction identified multiple high-affinity 9-mer peptides capable of binding to different human HLA Class I alleles. The selected epitopes exhibited excellent prediction scores and very low percentile ranks (0.01), indicating strong binding potential. These candidate CD8⁺ T-cell epitopes are suitable for further screening based on antigenicity, allergenicity, toxicity, conservancy, and population coverage before being incorporated into the final vaccine construct.

---

## Conclusion

The LptE protein of *Acinetobacter baumannii* contains several promising MHC Class I binding epitopes. Based on the IEDB prediction results, these epitopes were selected for downstream immunoinformatics analyses and will be considered for inclusion in the multi-epitope vaccine design.
