## Interpretation

The BepiPred 2.0 analysis identified seven potential linear B-cell epitopes distributed throughout the LptE protein. Several regions exceeded the default threshold score of 0.500, indicating a higher probability of antibody recognition. These predicted epitopes represent promising candidates for further immunoinformatics analyses, including antigenicity, allergenicity, toxicity, conservancy, and population coverage, before their inclusion in the final multi-epitope vaccine construct.

---

## Conclusion

The IEDB BepiPred 2.0 server predicted multiple linear B-cell epitopes within the LptE protein of *Acinetobacter baumannii*. These epitopes provide potential targets for eliciting humoral immune responses and will be evaluated alongside T-cell epitopes during the multi-epitope vaccine design process.
