# MHC Class II Epitope Prediction Results

## Protein Information

- **Protein:** LPS-assembly lipoprotein LptE
- **Organism:** *Acinetobacter baumannii*
- **Protein Length:** 169 amino acids

---

## Tool Used

- **Tool:** Immune Epitope Database (IEDB) MHC-II Binding Prediction
- **Website:** https://www.iedb.org/
- **Prediction Method:** IEDB Recommended
- **Epitope Length:** 15-mer
- **Allele Set:** Human HLA Class II Reference Set

---

## Objective

To identify potential MHC Class II (CD4⁺ T-cell) epitopes from the LptE protein that can stimulate helper T-cell immune responses and contribute to the development of a multi-epitope subunit vaccine.

---

> **Note:** The complete prediction results are available in **MHC_II_Epitopes.xlsx**.

---

## Interpretation

The IEDB MHC-II Binding Prediction identified multiple 15-mer peptides with strong predicted binding affinity toward human HLA Class II alleles. The selected epitopes exhibited low percentile ranks, indicating a high probability of effective presentation to CD4⁺ T cells. These candidate epitopes will be further evaluated through antigenicity, allergenicity, toxicity, conservancy, and population coverage analyses before inclusion in the final vaccine construct.

---

## Conclusion

The LptE protein of *Acinetobacter baumannii* contains several promising MHC Class II binding epitopes. Based on the IEDB prediction results, these helper T-cell epitopes were selected for further immunoinformatics analyses and will be considered during the construction of the multi-epitope vaccine.
