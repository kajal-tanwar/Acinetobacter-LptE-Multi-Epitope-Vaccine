---

## Tool Used

- **Tool:** Immune Epitope Database (IEDB) MHC-I Binding Prediction
- **Website:** https://www.iedb.org/

- **Epitope Length:** 9-mer

## Objective

To identify potential MHC Class I (CD8⁺ T-cell) epitopes from the LptE protein for use in multi-epitope vaccine design.

## Interpretation

The IEDB MHC-I Binding Prediction identified several peptides with excellent predicted binding affinity toward human HLA Class I molecules. The selected epitopes showed **very low percentile ranks (0.01)**, indicating strong binding potential. These epitopes are promising candidates for inducing CD8⁺ T-cell immune responses and will be evaluated further for antigenicity, allergenicity, toxicity, conservancy, and population coverage.

---

## Conclusion

The LptE protein of *Acinetobacter baumannii* contains multiple high-affinity MHC Class I binding epitopes. Based on the IEDB prediction results, these epitopes were selected for subsequent immunoinformatics analyses and inclusion in the multi-epitope vaccine design workflow.
