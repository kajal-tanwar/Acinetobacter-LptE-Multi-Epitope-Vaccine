# Multi-Epitope Vaccine Construct

## Overview

This folder contains the information and sequences used for the construction of the multi-epitope subunit vaccine targeting the LptE protein of multidrug-resistant *Acinetobacter baumannii*.

## Adjuvant

The 50S ribosomal protein L7/L12 was selected as the adjuvant component of the vaccine construct.

- **Adjuvant:** 50S ribosomal protein L7/L12
- **Organism:** *Acinetobacter baumannii*
- **Length:** 123 amino acids
- **Database:** NCBI Protein / GenBank
- **NCBI Accession:** ANA36502.1
- **Source nucleotide accession:** CP014266.1

The adjuvant sequence was retrieved from the corresponding NCBI protein record in FASTA format.

## Planned Construct Components

## Linker

The linker was selected for connecting components of the multi-epitope vaccine construct, following the construct design provided by the project mentor.

- **Linker:** EAAK,GPGPG,AAY
- **Length:** 4 amino acids


The vaccine construct is planned to contain:

1. 50S ribosomal protein L7/L12 adjuvant
2. EAAK linker
3. Selected MHC-I and MHC-II epitopes
4. GPGPG linker
5. Additional selected MHC-I and MHC-II epitopes
6. AAY linker
7. C-terminal His-tag (`HHHHHH`)

The exact arrangement and linker sequences will follow the construct design specified by the project mentor.
